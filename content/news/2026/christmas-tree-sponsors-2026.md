Our new look Christmas tree is lighting up Ashford, and we’d like to thank the local businesses who have helped make it possible.

A big thank you to [Walton Meats Butchers](https://www.waltonmeats.co.uk/), [Boyce Care](https://www.boycecare.co.uk/), W[alk On The Wildside Reptile & Aquatic](https://www.facebook.com/p/Walk-On-The-Wildside-Reptile-Aquatic-Ashford-61571447263954/) and [Ashwells Associates Limited](https://www.ashwells.net/) for their generous sponsorship of our town tree.

Your support helps bring a little extra Christmas spirit to our town, and we really appreciate it!

Thank you for supporting Ashford.
